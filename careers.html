Why Work at BHA

More Than a Job

At Benevolent House Academy, you are not just filling a role. You are helping build something from the ground up: a childcare center designed around the belief that every child deserves to be met with patience, purpose, and care.

❤️

Mission-Driven Work

BHA was founded by a family of nine children whose journey through foster care and adoption inspired everything we do. The work we do here is personal. You will be part of a team that genuinely cares about the children and families we serve.

🤝

Supportive Leadership

Our founders are hands-on and invested in your success. We value open communication, professional growth, and a workplace where your voice matters.

🌟

Growth Opportunity

As a new center that opened in May 2026, you have the rare opportunity to be part of our founding team. Shape the culture, grow with us, and build your career from day one.

Benefits

What We Offer

📈

Professional Development

We invest in our team. BHA provides ongoing training and professional development opportunities to help you grow your skills and advance your career in early childhood education.

🕒

Flexible Scheduling

We offer a variety of weekday shifts, and more options will open as we add weekend and evening care. We work with you to find a schedule that fits your life.

👶

Childcare Discount

BHA team members receive a discount on childcare enrollment for their own children. Take care of families all day, knowing your family is taken care of too.
