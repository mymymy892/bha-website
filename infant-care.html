Why Families Choose BHA

Trusting Someone With Your Baby Is a Big Step

We know, because we are parents too. Our infant rooms are built around one promise: your baby will be known, held, and cared for the way you would care for them yourself.

  • Small groups. One teacher for every 4 infants (6 to 11 months) and one for every 5 young toddlers (12 to 17 months).
  • Your baby’s schedule, not ours. Babies are fed on demand and nap when they are tired, following the feeding plan you write with us and update each month.
  • Safe sleep, every time. Infants always sleep on their backs in bare cribs, following safe sleep guidelines.
  • Formula and diapers included. We provide Similac 360 Total Care formula, plus diapers and wipes. No supply runs before work.
  • See their day. Brightwheel updates and photos let you see feedings, naps, diapers, and smiles while you are at work.
  • Fresh air. Our babies enjoy stroller walks on our walking path, as the weather allows.
Peace of Mind

Safety You Can See

  • Cameras record in every classroom and around the full perimeter of our building
  • Our front and back doors stay locked at all times
  • Children are released only to authorized adults, with photo ID checked for anyone new to our team
  • Every team member is background checked and trained in CPR and first aid
  • We are an egg and nut free facility
  • Licensed by the Texas Health and Human Services Commission, Operation #1832672. View our public record →
