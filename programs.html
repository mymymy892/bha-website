Our Approach

More Than a Classroom

At Benevolent House Academy, academics are just one part of the picture. We build our programs around three commitments that make BHA different from any other childcare center in our community.

🌟

Character Development

Each month, BHA centers on a character trait like kindness, courage, patience, or respect. Through classroom activities, stories, and recognition, children learn what it means to be a good person, not just a good student.

💚

Social-Emotional Check-Ins

Every day at BHA begins with connection. Our classrooms use feelings boards, calm-down corners, and relationship-building circles to help children name their emotions, feel heard, and build the self-regulation skills that last a lifetime.

🏠

Family Connection

We celebrate every kind of family. Our family connection practices honor the diverse structures of the families we serve. Every child sees their family reflected and respected at BHA.

Our Curriculum: Known & Growing

Every day at BHA, children are building, painting, singing, exploring, and discovering. Our classrooms are active, hands-on environments where learning happens through play, creativity, and meaningful interaction with teachers and peers.

Behind every activity is Known & Growing, the curriculum created by Benevolent House Academy and rooted in our mission. It places social-emotional learning and character development at the heart of each day, guiding our daily feelings check-ins, our monthly character traits, letter and early literacy learning, and original books and activities written by our own team. Each Friday, children bring home a portfolio of the week’s work so families can see their growth.

Known & Growing reflects what we most hope for every child: that they are known, valued, and growing in character, confidence, and a love of learning.

Nursery Program

6 Weeks – 11 Months  |  Ratio: 1 teacher for every 4 infants

The earliest months of a child's life are filled with rapid growth and discovery. In our nursery, every interaction is intentional. Our caregivers build warm, secure relationships with each infant, creating the trust that becomes the foundation for all future learning.

  • Individualized feeding, sleep, and diapering schedules based on each family's preferences
  • Rich sensory exploration through textures, sounds, colors, and movement
  • Daily tummy time, reaching, and grasping activities to support physical development
  • Language development through singing, talking, reading, and responsive interactions
  • Daily communication with parents about their child's day, milestones, and needs
  • Safe sleep practices following all SIDS prevention guidelines

Young Toddler Program

12 – 17 Months  |  Ratio: 1 teacher for every 5 children

Young toddlers are full of energy and eager to explore. They are taking their first steps, speaking their first words, and beginning to understand the world around them. Our program supports this incredible stage with patience, encouragement, and plenty of room to move.

  • Early language development through songs, stories, and responsive conversation
  • Movement-based learning: walking, climbing, and large motor skill development
  • Beginning social skills through parallel play and gentle peer interaction
  • Sensory bins, water play, and hands-on exploration of safe materials
  • Consistent routines that build security and emotional regulation
  • Transition support for children moving from the nursery

Toddler Program

18 – 35 Months  |  Ratio: 1 teacher for every 11 children (9 under age 2)

Toddlers are becoming more independent every day. They want to do things on their own, express their feelings, and make choices. Our toddler program embraces this stage by providing a structured yet flexible environment where children can explore, create, and grow at their own pace.

  • Language-rich classroom with storytelling, vocabulary building, and early literacy foundations
  • Art, music, and creative expression through open-ended activities
  • Social-emotional learning: sharing, taking turns, and naming feelings
  • Outdoor play and gross motor development every day
  • Introduction to classroom routines that build responsibility and self-help skills
  • Potty training support in partnership with families

Preschool Program

3 Year Olds  |  Ratio: 1 teacher for every 15 children

Three-year-olds are curious, creative, and full of questions. Our preschool program channels that natural curiosity into meaningful learning experiences. Through play-based instruction and our Known & Growing curriculum, children build the academic, social, and emotional skills they need to thrive.

  • Early literacy: letter recognition, phonemic awareness, and a love of reading
  • Early math: counting, sorting, patterns, and shapes
  • STEAM activities: hands-on science experiments, building, and creative problem-solving
  • Character development through monthly traits and classroom discussions
  • Social-emotional check-ins and calm-down strategies woven into every day
  • Art, music, dramatic play, and outdoor exploration

Pre-K Program

4 – 5 Years  |  Ratio: 1 teacher for every 17 children

Pre-K is the bridge to kindergarten. Our program prepares children not just academically, but socially and emotionally. Through our Known & Growing curriculum, we help every child enter kindergarten confident and ready to succeed.

  • Kindergarten readiness: letters, sounds, early reading, writing, and math concepts
  • STEAM projects that build critical thinking and problem-solving skills
  • Independence and self-regulation: following multi-step directions, managing emotions, making choices
  • Leadership and teamwork through collaborative activities and classroom jobs
  • Character development and social-emotional learning embedded in every lesson
  • Progress assessments and regular parent communication about developmental milestones

What Is Included

  • ✓Breakfast, lunch, and afternoon snack (USDA CACFP)

Registration and Fees

Registration Fee (one-time, non-refundable) $75 per child
Annual Supply and Activity Fee $100 per child

The supply/activity fee is due September 1st each year. Families enrolling mid-year pay a prorated amount. Tuition is due weekly, each Friday in advance of the following week of care. For full tuition rates by age group, visit our enrollment page.
