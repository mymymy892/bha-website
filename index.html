Your Child’s Safety

Peace of Mind, Every Single Day

You are trusting us with your whole world. Here is how we protect it.

📹

Cameras Everywhere

Cameras record in every classroom and around the full perimeter of our building.

🔒

Doors Always Locked

Our front and back doors stay locked at all times.

🪪

Photo ID at Pickup

Children go home only with adults on your authorized pickup list, and photo ID is checked for anyone our team does not yet know.

✅

Background-Checked Team

Every team member completes a background check before working with children.

❤️

CPR & First Aid Trained

Our caregivers are trained in CPR and first aid.

😴

Safe Sleep

Infants always sleep on their backs in bare cribs, following safe sleep guidelines.

🍎

Egg & Nut Free

Our whole building is egg and nut free, so mealtimes are safer for everyone.

🛡️

HHSC Licensed

Licensed and inspected by Texas HHSC, Operation #1832672. View our public record →
