Quick Reference

Important Reminders

Keep these key policies top of mind for a smooth experience at BHA.

9:00 AM

Drop-Off Cutoff

All children must arrive by 9:00 AM. Late arrivals require a doctor's note or prior written approval from the BHA leadership team.

Friday

Tuition Due Day

Tuition is due every Friday in advance of the following week. Autopay is required for all families and is set up during enrollment.

24 hrs

Symptom-Free Rule

Children must be symptom-free for at least 24 hours (without medication) before returning to the center after an illness.

$1/min

Late Pickup Fee

A $1 per minute fee applies after 6:00 PM. Please plan ahead and communicate if you are running late.

Photo ID

Pickup Requirement

Children are only released to individuals on the authorized pickup list. Anyone our team does not yet know must show a valid photo ID.

2 Weeks

Withdrawal Notice

A two-week written notice is required for withdrawal. Tuition is due in full through the final two weeks.

Red Oak ISD

Weather Closures

We follow Red Oak ISD. If Red Oak ISD closes, BHA is closed. On a delayed start, we open when Red Oak ISD schools open. Watch Brightwheel for every announcement.

Drills

Emergency Preparedness

We practice fire drills monthly and severe weather and lockdown drills quarterly. Our full Emergency Preparedness Plan is available for review at the center.

Egg & Nut Free

Food Brought In

BHA is an egg and nut free facility. Birthday treats must be store-bought, in the original package, and cleared with your child's teacher at least 3 days ahead.
